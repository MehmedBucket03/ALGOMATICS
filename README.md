## Overview

**Algomatics** is a web-based interactive educational platform that visualizes core concepts in **mathematics**, **data structures**, and **algorithms**. It bridges the gap between theory and intuition by offering animated, hands-on simulations for everything from **linked lists** and **binary trees** to **linear equations** and **systems of equations**.

Our goal is to enhance how students learn computational concepts—making them digestible, visual, and memorable.

---

## Final Features (Completed and Deployed)

### Core Functionality
- **Secure Authentication**: Register/Login with Firebase Auth
- **Continue Learning**: Resume your last topic instantly
- **Edit Profile**: Change email/password securely
- **Progress Tracking**: Auto-saves modules and displays in profile
- **Responsive UI**: Mobile and desktop support

### Visualizations Implemented
- **Linked List**, **Binary Search Tree**, **Stack**, **Queue**, **Hash Table**
- **Linear, Quadratic & System of Equations**
- **Arithmetic, Geometric, and Fibonacci Sequences**
- **Backtracking (Sudoku Solver)**
- **Sorting Algorithms**
- **Recursion (Factorial)**
- **Graphs and Pathfinding** *(Dijkstra’s, A\*)*

### Stretch Features
- Music toggle on pages
- AI Chatbot Assistant (partial integration)
- Email Confirmation
