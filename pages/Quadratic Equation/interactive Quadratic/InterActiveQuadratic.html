Explanation: Factoring Method

The factoring method involves breaking down a quadratic equation (ax² + bx + c = 0) into two binomials.

Steps:

  1. Find two numbers that multiply to 'c' and add to 'b'.
  2. Use these numbers to form (x + number)(x + number) = 0.
  3. Solve each factor by setting it to zero.
