What is Backtracking?

Backtracking is a way to solve problems by trying different options. If a choice leads to a mistake, you undo it and try another. It's like exploring a maze — if you hit a dead end, you turn around and try a new path!

Using Backtracking to Solve Sudoku

When solving Sudoku, the computer tries to fill a number. If it realizes later that no numbers work, it erases (backtracks) and tries the next option. This helps find the correct solution step-by-step!
